Who we are

Helping doctors find clarity in the in-between.

MedTransitions Global is a physician-led nonprofit organisation supporting medical students and early-career doctors through important transitions in their professional lives.

Our mission is to make accessible guidance, mentorship, resources and community available as doctors navigate key stages of their careers. We began in Ghana, where many career questions are answered informally through personal networks and the experiences of those who have gone before. We work to make that support more structured, accessible and responsive to need.

We see this as one meaningful contribution to the wider health workforce: connecting emerging doctors with experience, encouraging knowledge-sharing across generations and helping professionals make more informed decisions about their journeys.

About MedTransitions Global

Guiding transitions, shaping the medical future.

MedTransitions Global supports medical students and early-career doctors as they navigate important stages of their professional journeys.

Our mission

To equip medical students and early-career doctors with accessible guidance, mentorship, resources and community to navigate critical transitions in their professional journeys.

Our vision

A future in which medical professionals across Africa can access the guidance, opportunities and support they need to grow and contribute meaningfully throughout their careers.

Our approach

We start with the transition, listen to the people navigating it, and aim to make relevant knowledge and experience easier to access.

Our values

Clarity, Community, Excellence, Relevance and Equity guide how we intend to design and deliver our work.

Our work

Practical support for the next step in a medical career.

These are the areas MedTransitions Global is designed to work in. Specific programmes and activities will be identified as they are confirmed and developed.

Career transitions

Guidance for key stages such as moving from medical school into housemanship, early practice, postgraduate training and international pathways.

Mentorship

Creating opportunities for emerging doctors to learn from the experience and perspective of more established professionals.

Professional development

Accessible learning and practical information to support informed career decisions and continued growth.

Workforce contribution

Our longer-term ambition is to contribute to a more informed, connected and supported medical workforce. This is an aim, not a claim of demonstrated impact.
