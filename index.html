Who we are

Helping doctors find clarity in the in-between.

MedTransitions Global is a physician-led nonprofit organisation supporting medical students and early-career doctors through important transitions in their professional lives.

We began in Ghana with a simple observation: many of the questions that shape a medical career are answered informally, through personal networks and the experiences of those who have gone before. We work to make that support more structured and accessible.

Our programmes bring together doctors, specialists, mentors and peers to share knowledge, prepare for what comes next and build connections that can continue beyond a single event.
